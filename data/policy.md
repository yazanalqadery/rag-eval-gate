## Refunds
Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase. Refunds go to the original payment card within 5 working days. Gift card purchases cannot be refunded.

## Double charges
If a customer is charged twice, the duplicate payment is cancelled automatically within 48 hours. No action is needed.

## Subscriptions
You can cancel your subscription at any time in Settings. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the billing month.

## Login problems
If the app crashes on login, update to the latest version and clear the app cache. If you forgot your password, use the Reset password link on the login screen.
